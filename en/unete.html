BEFORE YOU TAKE THE NEXT STEP.

Who is the club for?

Students at Universidad Europea who want to get involved in a hands-on project. The official website welcomes applications; the team confirms availability in each area.

Is it only for engineers?

No. University sources also mention design and marketing alongside several engineering disciplines. Communications, logistics and management are part of the club’s work.

Can I participate in English?

Yes. FUEM is a bilingual club. Mention the languages you are comfortable working in so the team can guide you.

How do I apply?

Write to the team with your degree, year of study, areas of interest and availability. You can also use the application form on the official website.

What experience and commitment are required?

We have not found a current public requirement. Ask the club: needs and organisation depend on the season.
