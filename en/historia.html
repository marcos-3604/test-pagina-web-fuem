FROM A KART TO COMPETITION.

2007

Starting with a kart

The motor club is founded at Universidad Europea.

2008–2009

FUEM 01

The first single-seater is designed and makes its debut at Silverstone.

2009–2010

FUEM 02

The team competes at Montmeló.

2011–2012

FUEM 04

The project adopts E85 fuel.

2014–2015

FUEM 07

The final generation with a tubular chassis, according to the club archive.

2015–2016

FUEM 08

A carbon monocoque arrives, with events documented in the UK, Italy and Spain.

2017–2019

FUEM 10 / FUEM 11

Two further generations continue the project’s development.

2019–2020

FUEM 12

The next evolution is recorded in the historical archive.

2020–2021

FUEM 13

A season without competition because of public health restrictions.

2021–2022

FUEM 14

The club returns to competition.

2023–2024

FUEM XV / FUEM XVI

FUEM XV is displayed at Madrid Motor Student; the XVI design is presented in December 2023.

2025

FUEM XVII

The team enters Formula Student Netherlands and Spain with a new evolution.

2026

Continuing on track

FSS 2026 includes a completed Endurance event; the 2026–2027 season begins in the autumn.
