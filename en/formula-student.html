FORMULA STUDENT · MADRID, ES

THE CLUB / FORMULA STUDENT

MUCH MORE
THAN RACING.

A university challenge combining design, manufacturing, business and on-track performance. The car is just one part of the competition.

FROM AN IDEA
TO THE STOPWATCH.

University teams design and build a single-seater, then present it to judges in both static and dynamic events.

Before reaching the track, teams must justify their decisions, analyse costs and demonstrate that the car is ready to compete.

01STATIC

Engineering Design

Defend design decisions and explain the engineering behind the car.

02STATIC

Cost & Manufacturing

Analyse the car’s costs and manufacturing processes.

03STATIC

Business Plan

Present and defend a business proposal before the judges.

04DYNAMIC

Acceleration

Test the car’s acceleration along a 75-metre straight.

05DYNAMIC

Skidpad

Assess lateral performance on a figure-of-eight course.

06DYNAMIC

Autocross

Complete a fast lap of a technical circuit.

07DYNAMIC

Endurance

Evaluate reliability, consistency and performance in an endurance event.

08DYNAMIC

Efficiency

Measure energy consumption in relation to on-track performance.
