FORMULA STUDENT · MADRID, ES

01 / THE TEAM

TALENT
HAS A TEAM.

Since 2007, students at Universidad Europea de Madrid have designed, built and competed. A multidisciplinary, bilingual club.

LEARNING BY DOING

ONE SHARED
GOAL.

Formula UEM brings together students from different degree programmes at Universidad Europea de Madrid. We design, build and test a single-seater to compete in Formula Student.

The classroom gives us the tools. The workshop teaches us to make decisions, solve problems and work together. The track puts everyone’s work to the test.

INSIDE THE TEAM

EVERY DEPARTMENT COUNTS

DIFFERENT DISCIPLINES.
ONE PROJECT.

01

Powertrain

The heart of the car

Integrating the engine and the systems that turn energy into motion.

02

Vehicle dynamics

Every corner counts

Suspension, steering and vehicle behaviour: turning setup work into confidence on track.

03

Aerodynamics

Designing the invisible

Studying airflow and developing surfaces that work with the car in motion.

04

Chassis

The foundation of everything

Developing the structure and integrating components so the car works as a complete system.

05

Electronics

Connecting every decision

Sensors, data acquisition and electrical systems that help us understand the car.

06

Communication and management

The team beyond the track

Branding, content, organisation and relationships with the companies supporting the club.

07

Manufacturing

From design to part

Manufacturing and assembly processes that turn a design into a real car.

08

Logistics

Coordination that keeps us moving

Materials, resources and organisation to support the team’s work and travel.

LEARNING IN A BILINGUAL TEAM.

Formula UEM brings together students from different degrees at Universidad Europea de Madrid. Mechanical engineering, electronics, automotive engineering, design and marketing are among the disciplines mentioned by the University.

The club works in Spanish and English and welcomes the University’s international community. The project connects design, manufacturing, testing and communications with real competition experience.
